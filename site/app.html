Player Projections

Lineup Optimizer

Exact grouped-knapsack over the salary cap with slot partitioning — same algorithm as rgengy/optimizer.py. Lock 🔒 or exclude ✕ players on the Projections tab, then build lineups.

Contest Simulator

Monte Carlo over your generated lineups: each player is sampled from Normal(mean, sd), sd = √μ from Poisson variance. The synthetic field is a mix of optimizer-built ("sharp") and random entries. Results show projected cash rate, top-10, and ROI.

Run the optimizer first, then simulate contest outcomes.
